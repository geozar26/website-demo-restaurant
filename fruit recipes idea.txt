The Central Anchor & Grid:

Start State: A minimalist grid of high-quality fruit icons.

The Interaction: When a fruit is clicked, it stays in the center, and 4 surrounding cards (Top, Bottom, Left, Right) pop out.

Camera Logic: Implement a "zoom-out" animation so that when the 4 cards appear, the viewport expands to show all 5 elements perfectly centered without the user needing to scroll.

2. Visual Recipe Cards (The 4 Frames):

Background Images: Each of the 4 frames MUST contain a high-resolution, appetizing background image of the suggested dish.

Card Titles: Place the title (e.g., "Fruit Tart", "Smoothie Bowl") at the bottom of each card, using a dark gradient overlay to make the text pop.

Call-to-Action: Place a distinctive, pulsating purple "i" icon in the corner of each image to show it is clickable for more details.

3. Organic Connections:

Connectors: Use Framer Motion to create "organic," curved SVG lines that visually link the central fruit to the 4 cards. These lines should animate "outward" from the fruit.

4. The Recipe Detail View:

Animation: When a card's "i" icon is clicked, it expands or flips to reveal:

Time Info: Preparation and Cooking time with clean icons.

Ingredients List: A neatly organized list for easy reading.

Separators: Clear horizontal lines between Ingredients and Instructions.

Instructions: Legible step-by-step guide.

Video Footer: A dedicated placeholder for a "Watch Tutorial" video at the bottom.

5. Technical Preferences:

Stack: React, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.

Vibe: Clean, premium, and highly interactive.
